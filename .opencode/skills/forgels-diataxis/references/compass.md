# Compass

Source: https://diataxis.fr/compass/ (fetched 2026-10-18)

The compass is a truth-table/decision-tree that reduces the two-dimensional classification problem to two questions: action or cognition? acquisition or application? Use it when intuition gives no answer or a suspiciously easy one, on whole documents down to single sentences.

## Truth table

| If the content ... | ... and serves the user's ... | ... then it belongs to ... |
| --- | --- | --- |
| informs action | acquisition of skill | a tutorial |
| informs action | application of skill | a how-to guide |
| informs cognition | acquisition of skill | explanation |
| informs cognition | application of skill | reference |

## Terms (use flexibly, do not fixate on names)

- action: practical steps, doing.
- cognition: theoretical or propositional knowledge, thinking.
- acquisition: study.
- application: work.

## How to ask

- Do I think I am writing for x or y?
- Is this writing in front of me engaged in x or y?
- Does the user need x or y?
- Do I want to x or y?

Apply close-up (sentences and words) or wide (whole document). When a page troubles you, the compass forces a stop and reconsider: the content or the page usually needs moving to its true quadrant.
