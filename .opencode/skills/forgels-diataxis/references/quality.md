# Quality

Source: https://diataxis.fr/quality/ (fetched 2026-10-18)

Diataxis is an approach to quality. Quality splits into functional quality and deep quality.

## Functional quality

Accuracy, completeness, consistency, usefulness, precision. Independent of each other (docs can be complete yet inaccurate), objectively measurable, and readily apparent to users when missing. Attaining it takes discipline, detail, and technical skill.

## Deep quality

Feeling good to use, having flow, fitting human needs, being beautiful, anticipating the user. Interdependent, subjective (assessed against the human, not the world), judged not measured. Attaining it takes invention and taste, not just conformance.

## Relation

| Functional quality | Deep quality |
| --- | --- |
| independent characteristics | interdependent characteristics |
| objective | subjective |
| measured against the world | assessed against the human |
| a condition of deep quality | conditional upon functional quality |
| aspects of constraint | aspects of liberation |

Deep quality is conditional upon functional quality: no docs feel beautiful if inaccurate, or anticipating if inconsistent.

## Diataxis and quality

- Diataxis cannot GIVE functional quality; it can only EXPOSE its lapses analytically (e.g. mirroring code structure in reference reveals gaps; moving explanation out of a tutorial reveals unsupported leaps).
- Diataxis CREATES conditions for deep quality: fitting needs via user-based modes, preserving flow by blocking cross-purpose digressions.
- Diataxis is not all that is required: no formula, no shortcut past UX and design skill. It lays down conditions for the POSSIBILITY of deep quality.
